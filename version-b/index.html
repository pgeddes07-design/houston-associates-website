01

Accounting, tax & advisory

Monthly processing, bookkeeping, balance sheets and annual financial statements for small and medium-sized businesses. We complete and submit your ITR14 and ITR12 returns, work out IRP6 provisional tax, and handle SARS queries, objections, audits and verifications.

02

Payroll, done right

Your staff paid accurately and on time, leave accruals included. Monthly PAYE, UIF and SDL, your EMP201 every month and EMP501 twice a year, and IRP5 and IT3(a) certificates for every employee.

03

CIPC & statutory work

Annual returns, beneficial ownership submissions and compliance checklists, all kept current. Starting a business? We'll walk you through the statutory requirements and keep you on the right side of the Companies Act.
