01 · What we do

The numbers side of your business. Handled.

From monthly bookkeeping to a SARS audit, one team looks after it all. You get on with running the place.

Accounting & tax

Accounting, tax & advisory

Monthly processing, bookkeeping, balance sheets and annual financial statements for small and medium-sized businesses. On the tax side we complete and submit your ITR14 and ITR12 returns, work out IRP6 provisional tax for companies and individuals, and handle SARS queries, objections and assessments. Audit or verification? We prepare every document and schedule, whether it's VAT, PAYE, personal income tax or an IT14SD.

Bookkeeping Annual financials ITR14 · ITR12 IRP6 SARS queries & objections Audits & verifications
Payroll

Payroll, done right

Your staff paid accurately and on time, leave accruals included. Every month we run payroll and submit PAYE, UIF and SDL. We reconcile and file your monthly EMP201 and your bi-annual and annual EMP501, and prepare IRP5 and IT3(a) certificates for every employee twice a year.

Annual returns to CIPC, beneficial ownership submissions and compliance checklists, all kept up to date. Starting a new business? We'll take you through the statutory requirements and keep you on the right side of the Companies Act.

02 · Our story

Started with a favour. Grew by word of mouth.

It began in 1998 with a highly qualified accountant, a baby on the way, and a few friends who badly needed expert advice on their personal and company accounts. That accountant was Chanelle Houston.

More than two decades later, Houston & Associates is the trusted accounting partner for over 300 clients. Still small, still growing, and still all through word of mouth. In 2012 we were named a finalist in the 702 Small Business Awards for making a difference in our community.

Chanelle and her team of tax and accounting practitioners focus on one thing: the most efficient solution for every client. What we do matters for your business, and for the country's economy too.
